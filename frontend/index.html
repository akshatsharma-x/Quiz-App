Everything in one place

Built for how MUJ actually runs exams

From creating a quiz to analysing results, every step is fast, secure and real-time.

Focus-mode exams

Full-screen, distraction-free exams with a question palette, review flags, keyboard shortcuts and a server-authoritative timer.

Live proctoring

Tab switches, full-screen exits and disconnects are logged and streamed to faculty instantly, with automatic integrity flags.

Instant analytics

Students see scores, topic mastery and mistakes. Faculty get score distributions, question difficulty and CSV exports.

Cohort targeting

Publish to a program, branch, batch, semester or a single section. Students only ever see quizzes meant for them.

Question bank

Import hundreds of questions from CSV, tag them by topic and reuse them across quizzes in a couple of clicks.

XP & leaderboards

Every correct answer earns XP. Build streaks, unlock tiers and compete with your section, batch or the whole university.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

For students
  1. Sign up with your MUJ emailVerify with a one-time code — no admin approval needed.
  2. Set your academic profileProgram, branch, batch, semester and section decide which quizzes you get.
  3. Take quizzes & growJoin live quizzes, get instant results and track your progress.
For faculty
  1. Build or import a quizWrite questions, pull them from the bank, or embed a Google/MS Form.
  2. Schedule & targetPick the window, time limit and exact cohort — students are notified live.
  3. Monitor & analyseWatch the exam room live, force-end if needed, then review results and export CSVs.
